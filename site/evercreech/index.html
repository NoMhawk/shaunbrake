About Evercreech

Evercreechis a village and civil parish in Somerset, England. The village is 3 miles (4.8 km) southeast of Shepton Mallet, and 5 miles (8.0 km) northeast of Castle Cary. The parish includes the hamlet of Stoney Stratton and the village of Chesterblade.

The village was recorded as Evrecriz in the Domesday Book of 1086.[2]

Small Down Knoll (or Small Down Camp) is a Bronze Age hill fort above the village which rises to 728 feet (222 m).

Source “https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evercreech“

What To Do In Evercreech


Discover the charm of Evercreech, a quaint English village with a rich history and a warm community. Start by exploring the 13th-century St. Peter’s Church, followed by a visit to the Evercreech Nature Reserve for a nature walk.

Immerse yourself in local culture at one of the village pubs and relax on the picturesque village green. Wander the streets to admire historic architecture and explore local arts and crafts.

Check the events calendar for seasonal festivities, and don’t miss the opportunity to take countryside walks with breathtaking views.

Evercreech offers a perfect blend of history, nature, and community warmth for an authentic rural experience.
