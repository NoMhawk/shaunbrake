A flat roof covered in grey fiberglass presents a sleek and modern aesthetic while offering practical advantages. The smooth expanse of the grey fiberglass membrane creates a seamless, low-profile look that complements contemporary architectural styles.

The grey colour adds a subtle sophistication to the roof, blending well with various design schemes. Beyond its visual appeal, the fiberglass material ensures a durable and weather-resistant surface, providing protection against the elements.

This type of roofing is known for its water-resistant properties, making it a reliable choice for flat roof applications. The grey fiberglass seamlessly integrates into the overall design of a structure, offering a clean and uniform appearance while serving as a robust protective layer against rain, UV exposure, and other environmental factors.

A dormer window is a charming architectural feature that protrudes from the slope of a pitched roof, introducing both character and functionality to a home’s interior and exterior.

Typically characterized by its upright structure, a dormer window is designed to extend beyond the plane of the roof, providing additional headroom and space within the confines of the attic or upper floor.

This architectural element not only enhances the aesthetics of a building but also serves practical purposes, allowing natural light to flood the interior and offering an opportunity for ventilation.

Dormer windows come in various styles, from gabled and shed dormers to hipped and eyebrow designs, each contributing a unique touch to the overall architectural composition of a structure.
